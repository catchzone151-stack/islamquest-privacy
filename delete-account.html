IslamQuest - Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your IslamQuest account directly inside the app.

Delete your account in IslamQuest

  1. Open IslamQuest and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deleting your account removes your IslamQuest authentication record and associated app data from our active systems, including profile and learning-progress information, subject to any retention required by law or necessary transaction/security records.

If you used Sign in with Apple and the app cannot automatically revoke the Apple credential, IslamQuest may direct you to Apple’s instructions to remove IslamQuest from Apps Using Apple ID.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or need assistance with account deletion, contact info@catchzone.co.uk.
