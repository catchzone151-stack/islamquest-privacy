IslamQuest – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2026

IslamQuest ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data when using the IslamQuest mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use IslamQuest, we collect:

  • Email address (used for account authentication)
  • Display name (handle) and optional avatar
  • App activity data such as lessons completed, quiz results, streaks, XP, and progress
  • Technical data required for app performance and stability (such as device type and crash logs)

We do not collect:

  • Your real name (unless you choose to use it as your display name)
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Payment card information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save and sync your learning progress
  • To provide app features and premium functionality
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse
  • To improve app performance and reliability

3. Authentication and Data Storage

IslamQuest uses Supabase (a third-party backend service provider) for authentication and secure data storage.

Account credentials are securely processed by Supabase. Passwords are encrypted and never visible to us.

User data is stored in secure cloud databases managed by Supabase. Technical logs (such as IP address and device metadata) may be processed for security and system integrity purposes.

4. Payments

Premium purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play. IslamQuest does not collect or store payment card information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Data is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the app (such as Supabase for backend services and Google Play for payments), or if required by law.

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, associated personal data is permanently removed from our active systems, except where retention is required by law.

7. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time within the app.

Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication record and associated app data (including progress and profile information) from our systems.

8. Children’s Privacy

IslamQuest is not directed toward children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will promptly remove the information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
